# Carriage Run PC Demo Privacy Notice

**Draft — not approved for publication**  
Last updated: 2026-08-27

Carriage Run's Windows PC demo does not collect, transmit, sell, or share
personal information. It does not include analytics, advertising, player
accounts, online multiplayer, cloud saves, or automatic crash reporting.

## Information stored locally

The demo stores game progress and preferences on your own PC so that you can
continue playing and keep your settings. If the game crashes, it may also add a
technical message and source-code location to a local crash log. These files
remain on your device and are not automatically sent to us or anyone else.

On Windows, the files are stored under:

`%LOCALAPPDATA%\carriage_run\`

You can delete an individual save from the game's save controls. To remove all
progress, settings, backups, quarantined saves, and crash logs, close the game
and delete that folder. Uninstalling the demo may not remove local save data.

## External pages

A future release may provide buttons that open an approved store, wishlist, or
feedback page in your browser. Visiting those pages is optional, and the
operator of the page processes information under its own privacy policy. The
demo does not send your save data to those pages.

## Changes to this notice

If a later version adds an online service or changes what information is
handled, this notice will be updated before that version is released.
